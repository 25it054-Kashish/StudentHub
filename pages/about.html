Vision, Mission & Academic Philosophy

CHARUSAT has been conceived to put India on global education map by making Charotar the "Global Education Hub". The Faculty of Technology and Engineering (FTE) integrates rigorous theoretical fundamentals with cutting-edge industry frameworks, hands-on laboratory experimentation, and ethical technical stewardship.

🎯 Our Vision

To become a preeminent technical institution recognized globally for producing innovative engineers, impactful research contributions, and ethical problem-solvers who foster sustainable technological advancement.

🚀 Our Mission

To deliver transformative engineering education through interdisciplinary curricula, modern computing infrastructure, dynamic industry-institute partnerships, and an environment fostering curiosity and entrepreneurship.

🏛️ Constituent Engineering Institutes

CSPIT

Chandubhai S Patel Institute of Technology

Established in 2000, CSPIT is the pioneer institute offering NBA-accredited B.Tech, M.Tech, and Ph.D. degrees in CE, IT, AIML, EC, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering.

Est. 2000 • 6 Departments NBA Accredited
DEPSTAR

Devang Patel Institute of Advance Technology and Research

DEPSTAR focuses on future-ready domains including Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Cyber Defense, and Data Science with cutting-edge experiential labs.

Est. 2017 • Tech-Centric State of the Art
INNOVATION

CHARUSAT Innovation & Incubation Hub

A university-backed startup sandbox providing student founders with seed funding, intellectual property mentorship, server credits, and dedicated fabrication facilities.
